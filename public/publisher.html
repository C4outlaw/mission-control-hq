Myrie Publisher

A private desktop publishing tool for Myrie and authorized channel managers. Prepare approved media, choose exact destinations, schedule posts, and keep platform receipts on the local computer.

What the application does

  • Stores selected media and publishing jobs locally.
  • Connects authorized social accounts through platform authorization.
  • Uses YouTube API Services to identify the authorized channel, upload owner-selected videos and retrieve upload status.
  • Supports direct Facebook and Instagram publishing where permissions are granted. TikTok is handled separately through TikTok Studio; this is not a claim of approved TikTok Direct Post API access.
  • Shows each destination's result separately. Platform processing, account permissions and internet access are still required.

Account access

Google authorization requests YouTube read-only access to confirm channel identity and inspect upload results, and YouTube upload access to send approved videos. Access is limited to the account and permissions authorized by its owner. Myrie Publisher does not ask for your Google password.

This website describes the application. The publishing dashboard runs locally and is not exposed here. The application is not offered as an open public sign-up service.
