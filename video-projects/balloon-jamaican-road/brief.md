### Why it holds attention
1. **Suspense you can see:** a needle creeps toward a balloon in extreme macro. You stay to see the pop.
2. **On-screen countdown:** "FRAMES TO IMPACT 197 → 001" plus a REC camera HUD makes it feel like a measured event.
3. **Calling out the viewer while they watch:** a "watching 8.5s" timer and the lines "You just watched a balloon for 7 seconds… A balloon that doesn't exist… And you're still here, waiting for it to pop."
4. **The CGI reveal:** a wireframe mesh shows the balloon was never real.
5. **The pivot:** "Now imagine that was your brand," over a phone mockup of a feed.
6. **The payoff comes late (at 23 s), then the CTA:** logo, then "DM 'POP' for a free ad idea."

## The test
Change only the setting to **a Jamaican road**, so that's the one difference from the original. Keep the structure, timing and text the same; the brand and CTA at the end become Myrie's own.

## Setting
A Jamaican country road. Cracked asphalt with a faded yellow centre line and a deep pothole, a white route-taxi minibus with a red stripe (no real brand badges), a zinc fence painted green-gold-black, bamboo and breadfruit trees, green hills, a roadside jerk drum smoking, hot Caribbean late-afternoon light.

**The impact device:** the needle becomes the **front tyre of the route taxi**, rolling in slow motion toward the orange balloon sitting in the middle of the road, just past a pothole. (Alternative if Grok struggles: keep the needle, but hang it over the balloon on the road.)
