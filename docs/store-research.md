## Color rules (POD sales data, Printful/Printify 2026)

Black is the #1 selling garment color, white #2; avoid neon/mustard (worst sellers).
All top Jamaican sellers layer black/green/gold flag accents. Formula: black or white
garment + green/gold/red design accents. Exception: retro watercolor flag palette on dark heather.
