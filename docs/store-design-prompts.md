# The Lost Jamaican — First Drop design prompts (for Grok)

Run each prompt in Grok image generation. Ask for **high definition, flat graphic on a plain
background** (we need the artwork itself, not a mockup — Printify applies it to the product).
Target: 4500×5400 px transparent/white background for tees & posters; 2700×1050 px landscape
strip for mug wraps. If Grok can't hit exact pixels, generate at max resolution and we upscale
locally (4x) before upload.

Shared style notes: premium screen-print look, limited palette (black #0B0B0B, gold #F5C518,
green #009B3A, red accent #CE1126, cream #F6F1E7), no gradients-soup, no clip-art lions unless
specified, no misspelled patois (spellings below are final).

---

**1. not-perfect-mug** (mug wrap, black mug, gold/cream ink)
Typographic mug wrap design, the sentence "I'm not perfect but I am JAMAICAN and that's close
enough" set in bold stacked type; the word JAMAICAN twice the size, its letters filled with the
Jamaican flag pattern (green, gold, black diagonal cross); rest of type in cream. Flat vector
style, high definition, plain background.

**2. not-perfect-tee** (tee print, black shirt, gold ink)
Same sentence as a distressed screen-print chest graphic, stacked bold condensed type in gold
with subtle vintage crackle texture, the word JAMAICAN in flag colors. Flat graphic, plain
background.

**3. bomboclaat-mug** (mug wrap, white mug, black ink)
Dictionary-entry design: "bom·bo·claat" in large elegant serif, beneath it "/exclamation/" and
the definition line "used to express shock, surprise, or disbelief. See also: rhaatid." Classic
dictionary typography, black on white, one small gold star accent. Tasteful, minimal.

**4. lost-found-mug** (mug wrap, black mug)
Split design: left half a subtle halftone US flag in muted tones, right half the Jamaican flag
vibrant; across both in bold cream type: "LOST IN AMERICA" / "FOUND IN JAMAICA". Clean vector,
high contrast.

**5. wah-gwaan-tee** (tee print, black shirt)
"WAH GWAAN" in huge distressed retro varsity block letters, cream with gold outline, slight arc,
small "JAMAICA · EST. 1962" underline in green. Vintage 70s reggae poster energy, flat graphic.

**6. retro-flag-tee** (tee print, dark heather shirt)
The word "JAMAICA" in retro 70s script above a distressed watercolor Jamaican flag waving,
soft vintage sunset palette blended into the flag colors, worn screen-print texture. Flat
graphic on plain background.

**7. believe-mug** (mug wrap, black mug)
"Believe inna yuhself" in confident brush-script lettering, green with gold flourish, a small
rising-sun mark above the i. Motivational but rootsy, flat vector.

**8. dictionary-tee** (tee print, white shirt, black ink)
Minimal three-entry dictionary column: "Cho! /expression of annoyance/", "Rhaatid /mild
astonishment/", "Soon Come /arriving eventually, no promises/". Elegant serif entries, thin
divider rules, one gold accent bar. Museum-poster minimalism.

**9. area-code-tee** (tee print, black shirt)
"876" in massive gold athletic block numerals with green/black inline stroke, "LAND WE LOVE"
small underneath in tracked-out caps. Clean varsity aesthetic, flat graphic.

**10. never-lose-tee** (tee print, black shirt) — FLAGSHIP
"NEVER LOSE" in maximum-weight white block letters stacked two lines, tight kerning, a single
gold underline bar; tiny "THE LOST JAMAICAN" wordmark centered beneath. Brutally simple,
premium streetwear energy (think Represent/Fear of God lockup discipline). Flat graphic.

**11. more-money-mug** (mug wrap, black mug)
"MORE MONEY THAN LAST YEAR" in stacked bold gold caps, the word MONEY slightly larger with a
subtle metallic-gold fill, thin green rule above and below. Confident, clean, flat vector.

**12. cant-hear-poster** (18×24 portrait poster, cream paper)
Minimal typographic poster: "WHO CAN'T HEAR WILL FEEL" in huge black editorial serif filling
the frame, generous margins, small gold bar and "A JAMAICAN PROVERB" caption at the base.
Gallery-print quality, flat design.
